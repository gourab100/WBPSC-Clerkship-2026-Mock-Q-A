## Overview

**WBPSC Clerkship 2026 mock Q&A** ([https://wbpsc-clerkship-2026-mock-q-a-8916.vercel.app/](https://wbpsc-clerkship-2026-mock-q-a-8916.vercel.app/)) is an interactive study and solution dashboard covering **11 official West Bengal Public Service Commission (WBPSC) Clerkship question papers (2015–2024)** across both **Part-I (Preliminary Objective MCQ)** and **Part-II (Mains Descriptive)** examination stages.

All **524 unique parent questions** (**531 total prompts** including nested sub-parts `1a` / `1b`) are organized, categorized, and solved:
- **500 / 500 Preliminary Objective Questions** (`100/100` across all 5 shifts: 2024 Shift-1 `SLA/23`, 2024 Shift-2 `SLB/23`, 2024 Shift-3 `SLC/23`, 2020 Shift-1 `DBF/19`, and 2020 Shift-2 `BDA/19`).
- **24 / 24 Mains Descriptive Parent Questions** (`31` total prompts with sub-parts `1a` / `1b` nested inside their parent cards across Mains 2023–24, Mains 2020 Regular Set-3, Mains 2020 Group-A, Mains 2020 Group-B, Mains 2020 Supplementary Set-4, and Mains 2015).

---

## Key Features

- **Step-by-Step Mathematics Workings (150 Questions)**: Complete equations, algebraic derivations, and rendered **KaTeX** mathematical notation for every Arithmetic question across all five Preliminary shifts.
- **Concise Objective GK & English Solutions (350 Questions)**: Direct, verified answers and explanations for General Studies and General English.
- **Mains Descriptive Model Answer Guides (24 Parent Questions / 31 Sub-Parts)**: Structured outlines, key themes, word-count targets, and full high-scoring sample compositions in English and Bengali for Report Writing, Précis Writing, and Translation.
- **Interactive Practice Scoreboard**: Real-time **+1.00 / -0.25** negative-marking evaluation, accuracy tracking, and question bookmarking.
- **Bilingual Support & Embedded PDF Viewer**: Toggle between **Bilingual (`English + বাংলা`)** and **English-only** modes, switch between Booklet Series **A, B, C, and D**, and inspect the original PDF page for any question.
- **Paginated Feed & Scrollable Question Index**: View `20`, `50`, or `100` questions per page and jump directly to any question number (`1–100`) from the sidebar index.
